Data Science Intern

Nexvia

Worked on two applied data science projects, taking each one end to end: from raw data and feature preparation, through modelling and explainability, to an interactive dashboard that a business team can actually use.

DS-01 · Customer Lifetime Value & Churn Intelligence

  • Built a customer intelligence system on telecom customer data stored in PostgreSQL, using Python and LightGBM for churn prediction.
  • Used SHAP to explain which factors drive churn for individual customers and across segments.
  • Applied survival analysis (Kaplan–Meier and Cox models) to understand when customers are most likely to leave.

  • Used Darts MinT reconciliation to keep forecasts consistent across product and store hierarchies, and added prediction intervals to show forecast uncertainty.
  • Connected forecasts to inventory decisions with OR-Tools optimization.
  • Presented results in an interactive Plotly Dash dashboard.
